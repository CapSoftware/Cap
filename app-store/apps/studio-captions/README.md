# Studio Captions

Five caption styles you can apply in one click:

- **Headline**: big, bold, uppercase, with a yellow highlight on the spoken word.
- **Whisper**: small, light subtitles that stay out of the way.
- **Neon**: a bright pink pill follows every spoken word.
- **Broadcast**: solid bar captions for talks and explainers.
- **Mint**: a fresh green highlight with a gentle bounce.

## Use it

Open a recording in Cap Desktop, generate captions, and pick a style from the Apps row in the caption presets.
