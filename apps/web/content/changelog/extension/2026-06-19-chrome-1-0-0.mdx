title: Introducing Cap for Chrome
publishedAt: "2026-06-19"
version: 1.0.0
---

Cap is now available as a Chrome extension. Record your screen, a window, the current tab, or camera-only video without leaving the browser, and your recording uploads while you record so a share link is ready the moment you stop.

- **Tab, screen, window, and camera recording** - Capture the current browser tab, your full screen, a single window, or record camera-only video for quick updates.
- **Instant share links** - Recordings upload to your Cap library as you record, so the link is live the second you stop.
- **Camera bubble** - Keep a webcam overlay on the page while you record for walkthroughs and demos.
- **Microphone and camera pickers** - Choose your devices from the on-page recorder panel, with system audio where Chrome supports it.
- **Countdown before recording** - A short pre-roll countdown runs before capture starts, and you can cancel at any point during it.
- **Microphone check** - Cap warns you before recording starts if your selected microphone is not picking up any sound.
- **Draw on the page** - Turn on drawing mode from the recording bar to annotate the page while you record.
- **Quick dashboard access** - Jump straight to your Cap library from the recorder panel.
