- **Compact recording rail** - A draggable rail sits on the page while you record, with hover actions for pause, restart, and delete.
- **Camera resize** - Resize the camera bubble directly on the page, and dismiss panels without interrupting the recording.
- **More reliable starts** - Recording start and preview handling are more stable, and display capture retries automatically when the browser is slow to hand over the stream.
