---
title: Smarter Camera and Mic Permissions
publishedAt: "2026-06-22"
version: 1.0.1
---

- **Accurate permission status** - Camera and microphone status now reflects what Chrome has actually granted, so if access is reset you are asked again instead of seeing an empty device list.
- **Faster device checks** - Permission state is remembered between sessions, so the recorder panel shows your camera and mic status right away when it opens.
- **Standalone panel fallback** - On pages where the recorder panel cannot open, Cap now opens in its own small window so you can still start a recording.
