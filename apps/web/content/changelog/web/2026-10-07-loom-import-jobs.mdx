Moving from Loom is now a guided, live process. Drop a CSV or paste your links, check what Cap found, and watch every video move across.

## Features

- **2,000 videos per CSV** - Each import holds up to 2,000 videos. For bigger libraries, split the list into a few CSVs and run them side by side.
- **Checked before anything copies** - Cap checks every link with Loom first and flags private, password-protected or deleted videos with the reason.
- **Live progress for every video** - See what's queued, copying and done, with speed and time left. Close the tab whenever you like, the import keeps going.
- **Original titles and dates** - Imported videos keep their Loom title, length and the date they were recorded, so your library stays in order.
- **Try it on the free plan** - Upload your CSV and see your whole library checked for free. Copying needs Cap Pro, and the import starts as soon as you upgrade.
- **Loom to Cap link list** - Download a CSV that maps every Loom link to its new Cap link, ready for updating docs and wikis.
- **AI when it's watched** - Transcripts, summaries and chapters are made the first time each imported video is opened.
