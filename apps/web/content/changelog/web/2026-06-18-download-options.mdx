Downloading a Cap is now a proper menu instead of a single button, on both the share page and the editor.

## Features

- **Choose your quality** - Pick between the processed video or the original recording at full quality.
- **Download from the editor** - Grab a copy of the video without leaving the edit page.
- **Respects permissions** - Download options only appear for viewers who are allowed to save the video.
