---
title: Embed Caps Anywhere
publishedAt: "2025-06-17"
---

Caps can now be embedded on any website. Grab the embed code from the share dialog and drop the player into docs, wikis, or your own pages.

## Features

- **Embed code** - The share dialog has a new tab with a ready-to-paste iframe snippet.
- **Clean player** - Embeds show just the video player, sized to fit wherever you place it.
- **Copy or download transcripts** - Transcripts on the share page can now be copied to the clipboard or downloaded as a file.
