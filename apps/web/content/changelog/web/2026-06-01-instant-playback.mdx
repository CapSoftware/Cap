title: Watch Caps Before the Upload Finishes
publishedAt: "2026-06-01"
---

Share pages no longer wait for uploads. Instant recordings can be watched while the upload is still in progress, and viewers see a live preview even while you are still recording.

## Features

- **Playback right after stopping** - Open the share link the moment you stop recording and the video starts playing while the rest uploads in the background.
- **Live preview while recording** - Anyone on the share page during an active recording sees the latest footage as it arrives.
- **Seamless handoff** - When the upload completes, playback switches to the final video without interruption.
