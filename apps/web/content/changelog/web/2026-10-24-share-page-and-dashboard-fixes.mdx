A round of fixes for playback, captions, folders, and custom domains.

## Fixes

- **Playback after comments** - Playback resumes when a comment you were replying to disappears.
- **Timestamp links** - Timestamp links keep their position while the video is still loading.
- **Transcript search** - Searching a transcript jumps to the first result.
- **Captions** - Translated captions now include the final cue, and numeric captions are kept in transcripts and embeds.
- **Embed durations** - Embed players show hours for long videos.
- **Emoji reactions** - Unauthenticated viewers who react with an emoji are prompted to sign in.
- **Link previews** - Previews keep a recording's real dimensions.
- **Editor audio** - Preview audio is restored after short cuts.
- **Folders** - Video durations and edit controls now appear inside folders, adding Caps to a folder no longer creates duplicate shares, and subfolders stay in the same space as their parent.
- **Dashboard counts** - Video counts match the videos listed.
- **Analytics dates** - Daily analytics labels stay on their UTC date.
- **Custom domains** - DNS records always render in setup, the setup dialog no longer jumps to success, and removing a domain keeps it if the removal from the host fails.
- **Google Drive** - Rate-limited requests are retried instead of failing.
