title: A New Web Recorder
publishedAt: "2025-11-08"
---

The web recorder has been rebuilt from the ground up. It is faster, far more reliable, and a genuine option when you cannot install the desktop app or are on a lower-powered machine.

## Features

- **Screen, window, tab, or camera** - Choose exactly what to capture, with device pickers for camera and microphone.
- **Movable camera preview** - Drag your camera bubble anywhere while you record, with picture-in-picture support.
- **Recording controls** - A draggable in-progress bar keeps pause, resume, and stop within reach.

## Improvements

- **Reliable uploads** - Recordings upload in parts as you record, with automatic retries and live progress, so your share link is ready moments after you stop.
