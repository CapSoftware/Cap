---
title: Tighter Access Controls for Shared Caps
publishedAt: "2026-08-03"
---

A round of hardening across sharing and sign-in.

- **Comments require access** - Posting a comment now requires permission to view the Cap it belongs to.
- **Downloads respect sharing** - Downloading an organization video requires an explicit share, not just a guessable link.
- **Login codes expire on time** - Email login codes now expire after the advertised 10 minutes.
