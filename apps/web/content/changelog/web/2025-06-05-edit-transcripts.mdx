Transcripts are no longer read-only. Fix names, jargon, and mistakes directly on the share page, and your corrections show for every viewer.

## Features

- **Edit any line** - Click into the transcript to correct the text in place.

## Improvements

- **Player UX** - A round of usability improvements to the share page video player.
