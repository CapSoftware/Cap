publishedAt: "2025-05-29"

Two additions to your library today: lock any Cap behind a password, and upload existing videos straight from the dashboard.

## Features

- **Password-protected Caps** - Set a password on a video and viewers must enter it before watching. Even the title stays hidden without access.
- **Upload videos** - Bring existing recordings into Cap with the new upload button in your dashboard. Uploaded videos get share pages like any other Cap.

## Improvements

- **Player polish** - Chapters are better integrated into the player, alongside general UI tweaks.
