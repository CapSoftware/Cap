---
title: A Faster, More Reliable Loom Importer
publishedAt: "2026-10-24"
---

Loom imports now run on a dedicated importer that copies your videos into Cap quickly and finishes processing the first time someone watches.

## Features

- **Dedicated importer** - Videos are copied straight into Cap storage, and thumbnails and transcripts are prepared when a video is first opened.
- **Resumable large files** - Large Loom downloads upload in parts and resume if interrupted.

## Fixes

- **Bulk CSV import** - One failed batch no longer stops the rest of the import.
- **Stuck imports** - Imports that never started or failed part way are recovered and restarted.
- **Long titles** - Videos with very long titles now import.
