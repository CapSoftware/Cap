---
title: Private by Default and Viewer Invites
publishedAt: "2026-09-23"
---

Organizations can now start every new recording as Private, and owners can invite specific people to watch a private Cap by email.

## Features

- **Private default for new recordings** - Owners and admins can set new recordings to start Private. It applies to desktop, mobile, web uploads, Loom imports, and the API. Existing recordings keep their visibility.
- **Invite viewers by email** - Invite, list, and revoke viewers on any Cap. A signed-in viewer with the invited email can open a private Cap, even from outside your organization's domain.
- **Invited viewers in the share dialog** - See who has access at a glance.
