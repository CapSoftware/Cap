---
title: Shareable Link Limits on the Free Plan
publishedAt: "2026-08-20"
---

Free accounts now have a monthly allowance of shareable links, which keeps Cap free of spam while Cap Pro stays unlimited.

## Features

- **Monthly allowance** - Each month brings a fresh allowance for videos created that month. Videos over the limit stay locked until the owner upgrades.
- **Usage in the dashboard** - See how many shareable links you have used, with upgrade options when you run out.
- **Clear messaging for viewers** - Over-limit share pages and embeds explain why the video is unavailable instead of failing silently.
