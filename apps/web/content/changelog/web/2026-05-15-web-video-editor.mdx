You can now edit a Cap after recording it, right from the share page. Trim the start and end, split the video, and cut out the parts you do not want.

## Features

- **Trim, split, and cut** - Remove dead air and mistakes on a simple timeline, then save.
- **Rendered in the cloud** - Edits are applied server-side, so the shared video updates for every viewer with no re-upload.
- **Draft recovery** - Unsaved edits are kept in your browser so you can pick up where you left off.
