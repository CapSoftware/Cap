The dashboard now has a notifications inbox. See when someone views, comments on, replies to, or reacts to your Caps without leaving the app.

## Features

- **Notification feed** - Views, comments, replies, and reactions collected in one place.
- **Jump to the moment** - Click a notification to open the video with the relevant comment at the top.
- **Read states** - Notifications are marked as read when you open them.
