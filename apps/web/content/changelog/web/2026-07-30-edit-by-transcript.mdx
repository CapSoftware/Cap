publishedAt: "2026-07-30"

The fastest way to cut a video is now to cut the words. The web editor has a transcript sidebar, and removing text removes the matching footage.

## Features

- **Transcript sidebar** - The full transcript sits next to the editor timeline, synced word by word with playback.
- **Delete text to cut video** - Select words or sentences and remove them, and the cuts appear on the timeline instantly.
- **Filler word cleanup** - Filler words are highlighted so you can strip the "ums" and "uhs" in a couple of clicks.
