Migrating a whole team from Loom now takes one CSV. Export your library from Loom, upload the file, and Cap imports every video in the background.

## Features

- **CSV import** - Upload a Loom export CSV and Cap maps the columns, then imports each video with its title intact.
- **Runs in the background** - Large libraries import over time, with progress you can check from the dashboard.
- **Keeps owners intact** - Videos are assigned to the right teammates as they are imported.
