publishedAt: "2024-03-08"

The share page is now a conversation. Viewers can leave comments and react with emojis directly on any Cap.

## Features

- **Comments** - Anyone watching a Cap can leave a comment on the share page.
- **Emoji reactions** - React to a Cap with a single click while watching.
- **Editable titles** - Rename a Cap right from its share page.
- **Copy link** - A one-click button copies the share link to your clipboard.
- **Link previews** - Shared links now render a preview image in chat apps and social feeds.
