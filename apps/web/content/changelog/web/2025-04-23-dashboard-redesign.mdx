The dashboard has a new look. Cleaner navigation, a refreshed Caps grid, and better empty states across the app.

## Improvements

- **New layout** - A tidier sidebar and header make it faster to move between your Caps, shared Caps, and settings.
- **Refreshed Cap cards** - Thumbnails, titles, and actions are easier to scan at a glance.
- **Clearer empty states** - New pages explain what goes where when you are starting out.
