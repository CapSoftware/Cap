Captions are no longer stuck in the language you spoke. Viewers can translate captions on the share page player and watch in the language they prefer.

## Features

- **Caption translation** - Pick a language from the player and captions are translated automatically.
- **Context-aware output** - Translations use the surrounding transcript for better phrasing than line-by-line conversion.
