publishedAt: "2024-02-05"

Every recording now lands on its own share page. Send one link and anyone can watch in the browser, no account needed.

## Features

- **Share page player** - Caps play on a dedicated page with a seek slider, mute, and fullscreen controls.
- **Video thumbnails** - Links show a preview image instead of a blank frame.
- **Titles and dates** - Each Cap displays its name and when it was recorded.
- **cap.link short links** - Share links are available under the short cap.link domain.
