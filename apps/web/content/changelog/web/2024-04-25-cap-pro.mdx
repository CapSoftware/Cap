Cap Pro is here. A single subscription that removes the free plan's recording limits and supports Cap's development as an open source project.

## Features

- **Monthly or annual billing** - Choose the plan that suits you on the new pricing page.
- **No recording limits** - Record and share without the free plan's usage caps.
- **Promo codes** - Discount codes can be applied at checkout.
- **Manage billing in settings** - Update your plan and payment details from your dashboard.
