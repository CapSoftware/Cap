---
title: Live Transcription on the Share Page
publishedAt: "2026-08-03"
---

Transcripts no longer wait for processing to finish. Instant recordings are now transcribed while they upload, so the share page has a transcript ready the moment it loads.

## Features

- **Live transcript while uploading** - Viewers who open a share link mid-upload see the transcript appear live as the recording is spoken, then hand off seamlessly to the final version.
- **Instant transcript at load** - Landing on a finished Cap shows the transcript immediately instead of a processing spinner.
- **Live captions on the player** - Captions are available on the share page player as soon as they exist, without restarting playback.
- **Transcript tab front and center** - Landing on the share page right after stopping a recording opens the Transcript tab automatically.
