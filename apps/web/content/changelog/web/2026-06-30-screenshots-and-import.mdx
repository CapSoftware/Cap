Cap now handles screenshots as well as videos, and getting existing media into your library is much quicker.

## Features

- **Screenshots on share pages** - Screenshots captured with Cap get their own share pages and dashboard cards, just like videos.
- **Paste to import** - Paste a file straight into the dashboard and Cap imports it into your library.
- **Import any media** - The import flow accepts your existing videos and images, not just Loom links.
- **Record from Chrome** - A new dashboard button starts a recording with the Cap Chrome extension if you have it installed.
