title: Hosted Cap MCP Connector
publishedAt: "2026-09-22"
---

Cap now has a hosted MCP connector, so AI assistants can look up your recordings without running anything locally. Learn more on the [Cap for Agents](https://cap.so/agents) page.

## Features

- **Connect from your assistant** - Add Cap as a connector and sign in with OAuth. No tokens to copy around.
- **Read-only recording tools** - Assistants can list and read your recordings, and show an optional recording card in chat.
- **Revoke any time** - Connector access is scoped and can be revoked.

## Fixes

- **Claude and Grok compatibility** - The connector now connects cleanly from both.
