Organizations that handle PHI can now purchase a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) themselves, without going through sales.

## Features

- **Buy a signed BAA in settings** - Add a signed BAA to your organization from Organization Settings. It is billed with your plan and requires an active Cap Pro subscription.
- **Local currency billing** - Pricing is available in USD, GBP, and EUR.
