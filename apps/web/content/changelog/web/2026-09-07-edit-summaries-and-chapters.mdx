title: Edit AI Summaries and Chapters
publishedAt: "2026-09-07"
---

Cap AI gets you most of the way there, and now you can finish the job. Owners can edit the summary and chapters of any Cap right from the share page.

## Features

- **Edit the summary** - Rewrite or clear the AI summary from the Summary tab.
- **Edit chapters** - Change chapter titles and timestamps, and add or remove chapters. Player markers update to match.
- **Edits are kept** - Manual changes are not overwritten when AI results arrive late.
