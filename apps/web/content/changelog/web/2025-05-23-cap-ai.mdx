Cap AI writes the boring parts for you. After a recording is transcribed, Cap generates a title, a summary, and chapters automatically. Rolling out gradually.

## Features

- **Automatic titles** - New Caps get a descriptive name instead of a timestamp.
- **Summary tab** - A concise summary of the recording appears on the share page.
- **Chapters** - The player is split into chapters so viewers can jump to the part they need.
