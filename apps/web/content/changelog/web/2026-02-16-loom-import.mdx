Moving from Loom no longer means leaving your library behind. Paste a Loom link and Cap imports the video into your account, ready to share from a Cap link.

## Features

- **One-link import** - Paste a Loom video URL and Cap downloads and re-hosts it for you.
- **Everything works** - Imported videos get share pages, transcription, and analytics like any other Cap.
- **Free Loom downloader** - A standalone tool on cap.so lets anyone download their Loom videos, no account required.
