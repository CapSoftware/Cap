Caps are now transcribed automatically, and the full transcript appears alongside the video on the share page.

## Features

- **Transcript on the share page** - Read along while you watch, or skim the transcript instead of scrubbing.
- **Processing status** - The share page shows transcription progress and updates as soon as the transcript is ready.
