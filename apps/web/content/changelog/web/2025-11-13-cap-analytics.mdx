A full analytics dashboard for your Caps. See who is watching, where, and on what, instead of a single view count.

## Features

- **Views over time** - Charts of views across the last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or the lifetime of a video.
- **Audience breakdowns** - Split views by country, browser, operating system, and device.
- **Compare videos** - Drag videos side by side to compare how they perform.
- **Real-time tracking** - New views appear in the dashboard as they happen.
