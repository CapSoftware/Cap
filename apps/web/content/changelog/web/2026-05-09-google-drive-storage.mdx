Cap can now store your recordings in Google Drive. Connect Drive as a storage destination and new Caps upload straight into your own Drive, joining custom S3 buckets as a bring-your-own-storage option.

## Features

- **Connect Google Drive** - Link a Drive account and choose it as the destination for new recordings.
- **Organization-managed storage** - A new storage page in organization settings lets admins set where the whole team's Caps are stored.
- **Share pages unchanged** - Links, playback, and transcripts work the same wherever the video lives.
