Reorganizing your library no longer happens one video at a time. Select multiple Caps and move them together, or transfer an entire organization's content in one step.

## Features

- **Bulk move** - Select any number of Caps and move them into a space or folder in one action.
- **Move from anywhere** - Bulk actions work across your library, shared Caps, and spaces.
- **Organization content transfer** - Admins can transfer all of an organization's videos to another organization from the new content management settings page.
