---
title: Calls to Action and More Share Link Options
publishedAt: "2026-09-25"
---

Cap Pro owners can add a call to action to a shared video, and share links get new options for how the video opens.

## Features

- **Call to action** - Add a button with your own text and link as an end screen when the video finishes, plus an optional corner card while it plays. Choose a headline and color. Viewers do not need Pro.
- **Start with captions off** - Add `?captions=off` to a share link and captions start hidden. Viewers can still turn them on from the CC control.
- **Traditional Chinese translations** - Translate captions into Traditional Chinese, separately from Simplified Chinese.
- **Back to dashboard** - Signed-in owners get a link back to the dashboard on the share page, and the header now fits small screens.
