publishedAt: "2024-04-24"

You can now record a Cap without installing anything. The new web recorder captures your screen and webcam straight from the browser and gives you a shareable link when you stop.

## Features

- **Screen and webcam recording** - Pick a screen, window, or tab, and optionally add your camera.
- **Upload as you record** - Recordings upload in segments while you record, so your link is ready quickly after you stop.
- **Works alongside the desktop app** - Same account, same library, same share pages.
