A ground-up refresh of Cap for iOS ahead of its App Store release.

## Features

- **New dashboard and library** - Redesigned tabs with a cleaner shell and a floating glass tab bar for browsing your Caps.
- **Refreshed recording and uploads** - An extended record screen with clearer upload status, background upload queueing, and progress toasts.
- **Redesigned Cap detail and sharing** - A new detail view with quicker sharing, and shared Caps open straight into fullscreen playback.
- **Sign in with Apple** - Apple sign-in joins the existing login options.
- **Loom import** - Bring your Loom videos into Cap from your phone.
- **Account and analytics** - New account settings and per-Cap analytics screens.
