# Sources

- `gitleaks-rules.json`: the rules of [gitleaks](https://github.com/gitleaks/gitleaks) `config/gitleaks.toml` at commit 5f6ec56cfa6f, converted to JSON (id, regex, keywords, entropy, secret group, allowlists). MIT licence, see `gitleaks-LICENSE`.
- `first-names.txt`, `first-names-ambiguous.txt`: given names from the US Social Security Administration baby names and the 1990 US Census (public domain) and Wikidata given-name labels (CC0).
- `last-names.txt`: the 20,000 most common surnames of the 1990 US Census (public domain).
